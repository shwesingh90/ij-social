Holding an investment property personally or through a family trust is a decision that's hard to reverse after settlement. Our new article compares how each structure handles rental income, capital gains, land tax, borrowing and asset protection. Trusts can split income and shield assets from creditors, but losses stay trapped in the trust and land tax aggregation can bite in most states. Owning personally keeps the full CGT discount and simpler financing, but leaves the property exposed to personal liability.

For Australian investors adding a second or third property, it's worth modelling both options with your accountant before you exchange contracts.

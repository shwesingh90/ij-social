Our new guide offers a practical checklist for choosing property management software in Australia: rent collection and arrears, how the money is accounted for, the owner and tenant experience, maintenance and inspections, records and tax, and pricing and exit. A decision table shows where an agency with a rent roll and an owner-operator managing their own rentals need different things.

For Australian property managers and investors, the costly mistake is buying the wrong category of tool. Knowing how many properties you manage, for how many owners and with how many people, then testing one real property before committing, narrows the field quickly.

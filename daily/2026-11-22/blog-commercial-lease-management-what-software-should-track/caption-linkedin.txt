Commercial leases run longer than residential ones and carry far more negotiated terms, so our new guide sets out what lease software needs to capture: rent reviews (fixed, CPI or market), outgoings recovery, options, make-good, incentives, bank guarantees and the critical dates behind them all. It closes with a five-step test for evaluating any tool, and is upfront that dedicated commercial lease tools are on InvestorJoint's roadmap rather than built today.

For Australian investors and managers holding commercial property, the risk sits in dates: an option window or an expired guarantee can be missed months or years into a lease. Retail leases are also shaped by state and territory legislation, so the right setup depends on where the premises are.

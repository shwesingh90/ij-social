Can you pass water charges on to your tenants? Across Australia the fixed supply charge stays with the landlord, while usage can be recovered only if the property is separately metered and, in most states, meets water-efficiency standards. Each state and territory sets its own conditions, including whether the lease must say so and how quickly the tenant must receive a copy of the bill.

For investors with properties in more than one state, or held through a trust or company, getting these details right avoids absorbing costs that could have been recovered and heads off tribunal disputes. Our latest article walks through the rules state by state.

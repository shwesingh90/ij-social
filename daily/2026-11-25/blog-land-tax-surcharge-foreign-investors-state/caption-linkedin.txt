Foreign owners, and Australians living overseas, can face a land tax surcharge on top of standard land tax, and the rules are not the same in every state. Our latest article compares how NSW, Victoria, Queensland and South Australia approach it, what WA, Tasmania, the ACT and the NT do differently, and who counts as "foreign" or "absentee".

It also covers how revenue offices look through trusts, companies and SMSFs to the people behind them. For investors and managers with interstate or offshore exposure, it's a useful reminder to check structures and declaration dates before the next assessment.

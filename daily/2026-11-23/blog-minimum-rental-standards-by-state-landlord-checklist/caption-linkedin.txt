Our new checklist walks through the minimum standards a rental must meet in each Australian state and territory: smoke alarms, heating, locks and windows, ventilation and mould, water efficiency, and the essentials in kitchens and bathrooms. A quick-reference table shows where the rules are legislated and where they rest on tribunal case law, with Victoria the most prescriptive.

For investors holding rentals across more than one state, each property sits under different minimums and deadlines, and the obligation rests with the owner rather than the agent. Keeping photos, invoices and test records in a compliance folder for each property makes it easier to show compliance to an insurer or a tribunal.

Our new guide sets out the rent increase rules in every Australian state and territory: how much written notice a tenant must get, how long must pass between increases, and when rent can change during a fixed-term lease. It also covers how each tribunal can set aside a notice that breaks the timing rules or a rent it finds excessive.

For investors with properties in more than one state, this means juggling different notice periods and frequency caps at once. Tracking the last increase date and the earliest lawful next one for each property helps avoid an invalid notice and a tribunal dispute.

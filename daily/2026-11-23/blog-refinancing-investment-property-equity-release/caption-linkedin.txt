Our new guide looks at refinancing an investment property to release equity without muddying the tax position. It explains why the ATO looks at what borrowed money is used for rather than the property securing it, how split loans keep each purpose in its own account, and how redraws and mixed offset accounts can quietly turn deductible debt into private debt. It also covers lender serviceability and how refinancing costs are treated.

For Australian property investors growing a portfolio, equity release is a powerful lever, but only with a clean loan structure and a paper trail showing where each dollar went at drawdown.

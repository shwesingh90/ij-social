Our new explainer covers the four ways property management software is priced in Australia: per property, per block or unit, per user, and by quote. It shows how each model behaves as a portfolio grows, lists the add-ons that often sit outside the headline price, from payment processing and migration to e-signing, support tiers and contract terms, and sets out a simple way to compare vendors like for like.

For Australian property investors and agencies, the cheapest-looking plan can end up costing more once your real property count, logins and exit terms are factored in.

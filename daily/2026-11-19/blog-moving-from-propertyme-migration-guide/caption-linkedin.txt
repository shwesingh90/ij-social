Our new system-neutral guide treats a move away from PropertyMe, or any property management platform, as a rent roll cutover. It walks through what to export, why opening trust balances should come from a reconciled month-end, how to run both systems side by side for at least a rent cycle, what to do on cutover day, and how to brief tenants and owners before the change.

For Australian property managers, most migration problems come from skipping the reconciliation or switching mid-month, and with trust account rules varying by state, a planned cutover protects both the audit trail and owner confidence.

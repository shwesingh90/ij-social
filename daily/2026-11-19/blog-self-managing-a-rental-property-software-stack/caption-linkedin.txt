Our new guide breaks down what a self-managing landlord in Australia actually needs: a dependable way to collect rent, a single home for records, accounts ready for tax time, a trail for repairs, and reminders for compliance dates. It compares where a spreadsheet holds up and where it gets fragile, and suggests the order to set things up when moving to software.

For Australian property investors managing their own rentals, the admin grows quickly once a second property arrives, and getting the owner structure and records right early makes end of financial year a review rather than a rebuild.

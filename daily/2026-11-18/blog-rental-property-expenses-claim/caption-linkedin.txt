Our new checklist walks through the rental property expenses Australian landlords can claim, and the ones the ATO rejects. It separates costs deductible in the year they're paid, such as interest, rates, insurance and genuine repairs, from borrowing costs that must be spread over five years and capital works that are depreciated over decades. It also covers the limits on travel to inspect residential rentals, holding costs on vacant land, initial repairs, and the records to keep.

For Australian property investors and managers, classifying each expense correctly affects cashflow and the risk of ATO review, and it gets harder to track across multiple properties and entities.

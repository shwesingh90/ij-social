Repair or capital improvement? The answer decides whether you deduct the cost this year or depreciate it over decades.

1. Repairs restore; capital improvements enhance the property
2. Fixing defects present at purchase isn't deductible
3. Keep photos, quotes and itemised invoices as evidence

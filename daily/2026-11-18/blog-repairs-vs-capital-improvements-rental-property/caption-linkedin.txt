Our new guide explains how the ATO separates repairs from capital improvements on a rental property. Work that restores something to its original condition is generally deductible straight away, while work that makes the property better, bigger or different is treated as capital and depreciated. The article covers the initial repairs trap for new purchasers, when a like-for-like replacement tips into capital, common scenarios like painting, kitchens, flooring and fencing, and the evidence to keep.

For Australian property investors and managers, getting the classification right changes both this year's tax position and the cost base for the life of the property.

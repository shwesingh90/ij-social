Land tax is set state by state, and our new guide compares thresholds, rate scales, trust surcharges and exemptions across NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, the ACT and the NT. It also explains how aggregation adds together all the land you own in a state, which can push a multi-property portfolio into higher brackets, and why discretionary and unit trusts pay a surcharge in four states.

For Australian property investors and managers, the ownership structure and how holdings are split can have a direct dollar impact on annual holding costs, so it pays to review land tax alongside income tax and CGT each year.

Our new guide explains when interest on a rental property loan is deductible. The ATO looks at the purpose of each dollar borrowed rather than the asset securing the loan, so redrawing for a holiday or a home renovation carves out a private portion of the interest that has to be apportioned for the life of the loan. The article also covers offset accounts, mixed-purpose and cash-out refinance loans, construction interest, SMSF and trust borrowing, and the records the ATO expects to see.

For Australian property investors and managers, how a loan facility is used and documented directly affects the deduction claimed each year, so tracking the purpose of every drawdown matters.

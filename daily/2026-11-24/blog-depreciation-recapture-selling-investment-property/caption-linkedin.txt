Our latest article explains what happens to depreciation on plant and equipment when you sell an investment property. Under Division 40, if the value apportioned to items like carpets, blinds or air conditioners at sale is higher than their written-down value, the difference is assessable income and doesn't get the 50 per cent CGT discount. Capital works deductions on the building work differently: they aren't clawed back but reduce your CGT cost base.

For investors planning a sale, estimating this exposure from the depreciation schedule before accepting an offer helps avoid a surprise tax bill in the year of settlement.

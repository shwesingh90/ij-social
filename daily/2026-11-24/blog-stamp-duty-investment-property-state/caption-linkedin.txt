Our new state-by-state guide sets out 2026 transfer duty rates for investment property across every Australian state and territory, with worked examples on a $600,000 purchase. It also covers foreign buyer surcharges and how trust and company purchases are treated, including landholder duty rules.

Because investors don't qualify for first-home concessions, stamp duty is usually the largest upfront cost after the deposit, so the state you buy in can change whether a deal stacks up.

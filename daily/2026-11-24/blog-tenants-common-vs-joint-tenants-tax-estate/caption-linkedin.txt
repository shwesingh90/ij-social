Our new guide compares joint tenancy and tenants in common for investment property. Joint tenants hold equal shares and a deceased owner's share passes to the survivor regardless of their will, while tenants in common can hold any split and leave their share to whoever they choose. The ATO allocates rental income, deductions and capital gains by the ownership recorded on the title, not by who paid the deposit or services the loan.

For investors buying with a partner, sibling or business partner, the structure set at purchase affects tax each year and who inherits later, and changing it can bring stamp duty and CGT consequences.

A good owner statement shows what came in, what was taken out and why, what was paid, and what is still held, and the figures should add up with no unexplained difference. Our new guide sets out what each section should contain, the common errors that cost owners' trust (unlisted deductions, expenses without invoices, statements changed after sending, unexplained co-owner shares) and a six-point check to run before any statement goes out.

For Australian agencies, the owner statement is the owner's view of trust money, so one that doesn't reconcile to the ledger is a warning sign. Requirements differ by state, which makes a consistent, traceable process all the more important.

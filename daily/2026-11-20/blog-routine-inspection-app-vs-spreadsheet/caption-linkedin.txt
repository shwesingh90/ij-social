Our new guide compares paper and spreadsheet routine inspections with a phone inspection app across note-taking, photos, follow-up repairs and owner reporting. It also lists what a solid inspection record should contain, and when sticking with paper still makes sense.

For Australian landlords and property managers, the record matters most when someone later asks what the property looked like on a given day. Frequency, notice and conduct rules are set by each state and territory, so check with your tenancy authority before planning a schedule.

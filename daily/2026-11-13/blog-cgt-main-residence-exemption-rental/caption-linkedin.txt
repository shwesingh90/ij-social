Moving out of your home and leasing it doesn't automatically cost you the CGT main residence exemption. Our new guide explains how the six-year absence rule works, how moving back in restarts the clock, and the "first used to produce income" trap that catches owners who rent a property before living in it. It also covers the cost-base reset election and how partial exemptions are apportioned.

For Australian investors turning a former home into a rental, knowing when the clock starts and keeping good records from the first day of letting can make a real difference at sale time. General information only; speak to your accountant about your situation.

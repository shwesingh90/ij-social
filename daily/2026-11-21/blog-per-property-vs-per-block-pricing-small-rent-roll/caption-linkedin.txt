Our new guide walks through the arithmetic of per-property versus block pricing for property management software. Block pricing can mean paying a whole extra block for one new management, and a small agency below the first tier pays for places it doesn't use. The guide sets out a simple way to model total and next-property cost at your current size and where you expect to be in a year.

For small and growing Australian agencies, the price at today's portfolio size rarely tells the whole story; the cost of the next management and anything priced outside the headline figure matter just as much.

Our new plain-English overview covers trust accounting requirements for property managers across NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT and NT. It explains the four ideas every regime shares (a separate trust account, receipting, regular reconciliation and an independent audit) and summarises what each state's official pages say about receipts, reconciliation timing, audits and who is responsible.

For agencies managing across states, the practical rules diverge more than the principles, so the guide suggests building a short checklist per state from the regulator's own guidance. General information, not legal advice.

Our new guide sets out the general rent arrears escalation ladder in Australia: early contact, a written reminder, a payment plan where it's realistic, a formal notice, and only then a tribunal or court application. It includes a state-by-state table of the decision-maker, notice trigger and who enforces a possession order, checked against official pages in November 2026.

For Australian property managers and investors, a wrong form or a miscounted day can invalidate a notice, so the guide is a starting point to confirm each step with your state's tenancy authority. General information, not legal advice.

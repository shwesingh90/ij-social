Task management is now live in InvestorJoint.

Property work rarely lives in one place: a lease renewal in your inbox, a compliance check on a spreadsheet, a maintenance follow-up on a sticky note. Now it can all sit in one place, right next to the properties, leases and clients it belongs to.

What it does:
1. Board or list view, with lanes you name yourself and drag-and-drop between them
2. Rich task cards: several assignees and followers, checklists where each item has its own owner and due date, coloured labels, start and due dates, files, comments and a full history
3. Custom fields (text, number, amount, date, dropdown, person or property) that show on cards and can be filtered
4. Tasks linked to a property, entity or contact, or created straight from an email
5. Workflows: staged steps assigned by role with due dates, set to repeat monthly, quarterly or yearly, or started automatically when an application is approved, a lease is signed, an offer is accepted or a settlement date is set
6. Saved filters shared with your team, bulk edits, task templates and copying
7. Bell and email alerts when you're assigned, and reminders when work is due tomorrow or overdue

Find it under Tasks in the sidebar. Your first property is free: investorjoint.com.au

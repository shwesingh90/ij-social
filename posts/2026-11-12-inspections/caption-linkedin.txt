Inspections are where property records are won or lost.

A missed photo at move-in becomes a bond dispute at move-out. A routine inspection logged in someone's notes app is hard to find a year later.

InvestorJoint now runs every inspection, for every property, from one list:

1. Create: pick the property and lease, choose the type (incoming, ongoing or end of lease), then schedule it or start on the spot.
2. Walk through: rooms are already set up. Rate the condition and cleanliness of each one, and add notes and photos. Everything auto-saves.
3. Complete: track each inspection from scheduled to completed, mark it done and download the report.

End-of-lease inspections use the same rooms as the incoming one, so the two can be compared side by side.

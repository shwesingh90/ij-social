One house. Many rooms. Each one its own rental. 🏠

With InvestorJoint, a rooming house is managed room by room:

1️⃣ Separate lease: every room has its own lease and its own tenant
2️⃣ Separate rent: set and track rent for each room on its own
3️⃣ Room details: type, furnishing, bathroom, features and availability

When one tenant moves out, the rest of the house stays exactly as it is.

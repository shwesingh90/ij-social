Rooming houses don't fit the "one property, one lease" mould.

Each room has a different tenant, a different move-in date and a different rent. Squeeze all of that into a single lease or a spreadsheet and the details get lost fast.

InvestorJoint treats every room as its own rental:

1. Separate lease: each room gets its own lease with its own tenant, so one tenant moving on never means redoing the whole house.
2. Separate rent: set and track rent room by room.
3. Room details: record each room's type, furnishing, bathroom, features and availability, so you can see what's free and what each room offers.

All the rooms sit under one property, so the whole house stays in one place.

Built for landlords and investors running rooming houses and rent-by-the-room properties.
